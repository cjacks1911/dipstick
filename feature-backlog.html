The Competition

6 apps · pricing & the gap each one leaves

Pulled from product pages and side-by-side reviews in August 2026. "Gap" is the complaint or missing piece that shows up across multiple independent write-ups, not a guess.

CARFAX Car Care
Free · 50M+ users
Gap: rigid intervals (tire rotation locked to 5k/7.5k), penalizes independent shops with a "well-maintained badge" system, edits silently revert to "Self Service."
Drivvo
Free + Premium subscription
Gap: no part-number tracking or photo attachments; cloud sync gated behind premium; a recent price hike drew complaints.
Simply Auto
Free + $0.99–9.99/mo
Gap: no parts inventory, no AI/diagnosis; interface reviewers called "utilitarian" and "dated."
Fuelly / aCar
Free + Pro tier
Gap: fuel-tracking specialists — basic reminders only, no parts, no photos, not a service replacement.
AUTOsist
Free (1 car) · fleet tiers beyond
Gap: built for fleets — dash cams, driver scoring, DOT inspections. Personal-garage features (documents, parts) are buried under compliance tooling.
FIXD / OBDeleven
Free app + $20–200 hardware
Gap: real diagnostics, but only with a Bluetooth dongle in the car — nothing works without buying hardware first.

New · v1.2
Light/dark theme toggle
A sun/moon button in the header switches the whole app between dark and light palettes, remembered per browser. Defaults to matching the OS setting until you pick one explicitly — no flash of the wrong theme on load.
off the bench, on request
New · v1.3
Insurance claim log
A dedicated record for accidents and claims, kept separate from routine maintenance — category, insurer, what happened, claimed/deductible/paid amounts, and a filed/approved/denied/closed status. CSV export like the other logs.
off the bench, on request
New · v1.4
Parts inventory with low-stock alerts
Garage-wide stock of spares on the shelf — not tied to one vehicle, unlike the per-vehicle Parts tab which tracks what's already installed. Set a quantity and reorder threshold, adjust with +/- as you use or restock, and it surfaces in the same reminders feed once you're low or out.
off the bench, scoped down from AUTOsist's fleet-stock model
New · v1.5
Multi-currency support
Pick from 12 common currencies in Settings and every cost across the garage relabels immediately — fleet stats, cost/mile, every log, the printable record. Building it also caught and fixed a real bug: dashboard cost-per-mile was always rounding to $0/mi.
off the bench, on request
New · v1.6
Fleet-wide spend chart
A 12-month bar chart of maintenance + fuel + parts across every vehicle at once, hand-drawn as inline SVG — no charting library. Complements the per-vehicle lifetime total already on the Cost tab with the "is this trending up" view.
off the bench, on request
New · v1.7
Repair Age
A car's condition age next to its calendar age — five signals already sitting in your own logs (service adherence, fault codes, insurance claims, mileage pace, fuel-efficiency trend), gated off entirely below 3 of 5 rather than guessing. Nobody in the six scanned apps has anything like it — it's not from the competitor scan, it's Dipstick's own formula. It's also plain JavaScript in a static site, same as everything else here: nothing about it is hidden from view-source, and what makes it worth having was never secrecy — it's that copying the number means copying years of one car's real history, which nobody else has.
not off the bench — a new idea, on request
New · v1.8
Mileage check-ins + GPS odometer suggestion
Every reminder in this app — service due, Repair Age, mileage pace — is only as honest as the odometer figure behind it, so Dipstick now tracks when that figure was last actually confirmed and nudges again roughly twice a month if it's gone stale, right in the existing "Coming up" feed and its opt-in notifications. The Trips tab's GPS logging now also sums distance since your last confirmed reading and offers a one-click update — a suggestion to confirm, not an automatic overwrite, since GPS drifts a little and only runs while the tab's open.
not off the bench — a new idea, on request

Deliberately Not Building

needs a live backend or a commercial data deal
Out of scope

Dipstick has no server, by design — the same standing rule HUSLLYFE was built under. Hosting on real HTTPS (Netlify) fixed two earlier cuts that only needed a secure browser context — OBD2 and GPS trip logging moved up to "Shipped." What's left below needs something hosting alone can't provide: a live backend or a data partnership.
